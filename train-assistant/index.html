JAPAN LIFE · TRAIN & TRANSIT

Travel across Japan.
One step simpler.

Find stations, plan public transport routes, understand station announcements and get practical travel guidance in one mobile-friendly app.

01 · Plan your train journey

TRANSIT ROUTE

Enter station names or places. Directions open in Google Maps with public transport selected; the external map calculates live routes, times and fares when provided.

We do not invent exact fares, departure times, platform numbers or delays.

02 · Find a nearby station

OPENSTREETMAP

Use your location (optional) to look up nearby railway stations from OpenStreetMap via Overpass. Results depend on volunteer map data and network availability.

🗺️ Open maps search

Location stays in your browser until a lookup request; coordinates are sent to the public Overpass service only when you press the button.

03 · Understand Japanese announcements

SMART HELPER

Paste a Japanese sign or announcement. A small on-device phrase matcher explains common railway terms in Bangla or English. It is not a full AI translator and may miss critical details.
