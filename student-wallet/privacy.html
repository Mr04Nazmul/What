Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Student Wallet Pro (the “App”) handles information. The App is a personal budget, part-time work shift, and savings tracker for students. It is not a banking service and does not connect to or manage bank accounts.

Information you enter

You may enter income, expenses, transaction descriptions and dates, part-time work shifts, hourly pay, tuition goals, savings amounts, language and appearance settings. The current App stores these records locally on your device using web storage. We do not currently provide an account or operate a server that receives this wallet data. Data is not automatically synced between devices.

Backups and deletion

You can export a JSON backup of your records using the App's backup feature. A backup is saved where you choose and is your responsibility to protect. You can delete your local records by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App; clearing browser site data also removes web-version records. Please export a backup first if you need to keep your records.

Advertising

The Android version may use Google AdMob to display interstitial advertisements and advertisements when opening or returning to the App. Google Mobile Ads SDK and related consent services may collect or process identifiers (including advertising identifiers where permitted), device and app information, ad interactions, diagnostics, approximate location inferred from network information, and other information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, depending on your device, location, permissions, and privacy choices. Such processing is subject to Google's privacy policies. The App may ask for advertising consent where required. Ads may not appear in all builds or regions; debug builds use test ads.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

Website and hosting

The web version is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and its infrastructure may process ordinary technical access data such as IP addresses and request metadata to operate and protect the website. We do not use this hosting to store your personal transaction entries.

Sharing

We do not sell your manually entered income, spending, or savings records. Advertising and hosting providers may independently process technical information as explained above. We do not intentionally transmit your financial entries to advertising providers.

Children

The App is intended for people old enough to manage their own budgeting information in accordance with applicable law. Users should not enter sensitive account credentials or identifying financial documents in transaction notes.

Security

Your locally stored information can be accessed by someone who has access to your unlocked device or browser profile. Protect your device and any exported backup files. No system is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if features, hosting, or advertising practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact the project maintainer through the Student Wallet Pro GitHub issues page. Please do not include private financial details in public issues.
